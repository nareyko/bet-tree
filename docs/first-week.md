# First week

One page for a founder who has not lived a Bet Tree week yet. Read [the method](method.md) sections 1–8 if a term is unclear. Keep unknowns as **unknown**.

## Before the first Monday morning

1. Copy three blanks: [01-bet](../templates/01-bet.md), [05-week](../templates/05-week.md), [07-journal](../templates/07-journal.md).
2. Keep [08-meeting](../templates/08-meeting.md) open for Monday morning and Friday morning.
3. On the bet sheet, write one desired future three ways (forecast, goal, bet). Keep only the bet. Name what is known and what is unknown.
4. Choose the kind of future, or write **research project / value check**. Write what you put in and what you can lose.
5. Build a short L-frame. At the very start, three Business horizons are enough: 1 year, month, and week. Leave Quarter and 3–5 years as unknown until you have something to write (a qualitative picture counts). Write the word unknown in the cell.
6. On the week sheet, write the week's bet and personal promises (or Reach, Build, Contact if you work alone).
7. Add sample `n` out of `N`, success threshold, guardrail, and the hour you will look at the result. In the teaching rhythm that hour is **next Monday morning**, seven days later.

Do not invent a number to fill a blank. Write **unknown**.

## First Monday morning (~20–25 minutes)

There is no past week to judge yet. Use the meeting checklist for **open the next seven days** only: one constraint, one leading lever, success threshold, guardrail, next Monday morning as the look date.

Week zero: if you have no contacts and no numbers yet, spend the barrier item on what blocked the first live contact, then still write threshold and guardrail.

## During the seven days

Do the actions you promised. Do not rewrite the threshold after you see early replies. Keep the guardrail. Move the first lever early in the week so a signal can arrive before the next Monday.

## Friday morning (~15–20 minutes)

Mid-week check only. Read facts. Note sample progress. Confirm the guardrail. List hypothesis options for Monday's possible paths. Do not mark one of the four outcomes. Do not change the threshold. Leave the options for the weekend.

## Second Monday morning (~20–25 minutes)

Close the past seven days: facts, one of four outcomes, journal row. Then open the next seven days on a new week sheet (or a clear new plan block): constraint, lever, threshold, guardrail, next Monday morning again.

## What not to do in week one

- Do not open the Advanced sheets (causal map, focus, passport, scorecard).
- Do not change the product because the sample is still small. Finish the same sample.
- Do not treat classroom examples as your market evidence.
- Do not turn Friday into a decision meeting.
