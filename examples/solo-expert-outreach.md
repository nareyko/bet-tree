# Teaching example: solo expert outreach

This is a classroom week for one person who sells their own expertise (advice, review, or a short paid call). Every number below is a **bet written before experience**. None of it is market evidence, a measured result, or a price recommendation.

Use only the Basic blanks for this example: bet, week sheet, journal, meeting checklist. Do not open Advanced sheets.

## Kind of future

Status: `research project / value check`. The founder wants to know whether named people will take a next step after a short message. Revenue for the year stays **unknown**.

## L-frame (3–5 year Founder stake, then nearer bridges)

This week is a subproject under a longer stake. The living check is one week. The far cell here is Founder (the life the founder wants); Business revenue path stays unknown.

| Horizon | Stake (pre-evidence) |
| --- | --- |
| 3–5 years (Founder) | Independent practice where paid expert work is a stable part of the founder's working life (qualitative picture) |
| 1 year (Founder and Business bridge) | At least one paid step that the founder can name without borrowing a stranger's price |
| Month | At least 2 clear yes/no replies from a planned sample of 5 named contacts |
| Week | Send 5 personal messages; get at least 2 replies that name a next step or a clear refusal |

Customer this week: five people the founder already knows by name. Product: one short offer sentence and a time window. Founder this week: at most 5 hours on outreach (serves the far Founder stake).

Link to the month: if 2 of 5 reply with a next step or a clear refusal, the month sample continues with the same offer. If silence fills the sample, the offer sentence changes next week — not the product suite.

## Week contract

**Bet.** If I send one clear offer to 5 named contacts this week, at least 2 will reply with a next step or a refusal in their own words by next Monday morning.

**Actions.** Reach: send 5 messages. Build: keep the offer to one sentence. Contact: answer each reply the same day.

**Sample and threshold.** `n` = 5 out of `N` = 5. Win: at least 2 replies with a next step or a clear refusal (not silence).

**Guardrail.** Spend 0 on ads. Do not invent a price in the message unless the contact asks. Look on next Monday morning (seven days).

**Unknown.** Conversion to payment, fee level, and whether a second stranger will appear without a personal ask — all **unknown**.

## Friday morning mid-week check

Read facts and sample progress. List hypothesis options for Monday. Do not mark one of the four outcomes.

## Next Monday morning

Mark one outcome from the four in the method. Write what you learned and what surprised you. Then write next week's bet. Do not treat classroom numbers as your market.

## What this example is not

- Not a sales playbook.
- Not proof that 2 of 5 is a good rate.
- Not a substitute for a real week with your own contacts.
