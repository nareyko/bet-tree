# Prompts for the advanced level

Use these only after a basic week has been lived. The assistant does not invent numbers. Unknown stays **unknown**.

If several subprojects share one long bet, keep advanced sheets on the **main** line (or the subproject that owns Monday). Roles and attention quotas stay in [portfolio.md](portfolio.md) and blank 09.

## 1. Horizons

```
Study the idea, the project documents, and the current bet document. Separate a checked fact from an assumption.

Show the current 3–5 year draft and offer ways to sharpen it. Wait for my choice.

After that choice, offer bets for the year, the quarter, the month, and the week. Wait for my decision at each step.

For each bet, record the result, the threshold, the date, the type, and how proved it is. Mark anything unknown with the word "unknown".

Save what I chose in the bet document. Do not change other documents.
```

## 2. Causal links

```
Study the bet document and the project materials.

Offer causal links between bets and categories. For each, write the mechanism, the delay, and the evidence that already exists.

Ask about one link at a time and wait for my decision: confirm, change, or record as unknown.

Save the chosen links and the way to check them in the causal map. Do not change other documents.
```

## 3. Constraint and focus

```
Study the bet document, the causal map, and the week materials. Use this only after a basic week, when the link map is visible.

Help me fill the focus sheet:
- the cycle aim;
- exactly one current constraint (one bottleneck);
- the main metric of that constraint;
- the week threshold;
- one action for this week;
- what we will not do this month (four sides: money, customer, process, what we learned).

Show the draft. After I confirm, save it to the focus sheet. Do not change other documents.
```

## 4. Metric passport

```
Study the bet, the focus sheet, and any metric draft. Check one number before it enters the scorecard.

Help me fill the metric passport. First ask: what question does this number answer? Wait. Then six required fields:
1. Link to the bet.
2. Role relative to the result (leading, lagging, or guardrail).
3. Definition, formula, and source (what counts, what does not, record source, definition version and date).
4. Action before the next meeting.
5. Threshold, reaction rule, and decision.
6. Owner and attention cost.

Also record strength of evidence (said / did / paid) and one verdict: keep on the weekly scorecard / diagnostic only / temporary 1–2 week scouting indicator / drop.

Remind me: do not drop a new metric after the first week. Check it for two or three weeks unless a guardrail fired. Run the eight short checks of a number from the method before the verdict.

Never propose a threshold, a formula result, or an owner name. Ask me. If I do not know, write **unknown**.

Show the draft. After I confirm, save the passport. Do not change other documents.
```

## 5. Scorecard

```
Study the bet, the metric passport, and the week plan. Build the scorecard only after the passport fields are filled.

Build a weekly scorecard:
- the month's aim;
- a table of 3 to 5 measures: name, role (leading, lagging, or guardrail), this week's fact, threshold, status ("on plan", "off plan", or "still collecting"), and owner.

Show the draft. After I confirm, save the scorecard. Do not change other documents.
```
